Delete your data

Last updated: 25 August 2026 · App: com.sagearbor.mindshift.app

In one line. You can delete individual recordings and your voiceprint yourself, from inside the app, right now. To delete your whole account and everything under it, email us — we do it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Delete individual things, in the app

  • A recording or session — open Recordings, pick the session, tap Delete. This removes its stored audio, its video, its transcript and its analysis, and revokes every share on it.
  • Your voiceprint — Settings → Advanced → Forget my voice. Your voice signature is erased permanently. There is no tombstone and no backup copy.
  • One enrolled person — Settings → People, pick the person, delete. Removes that person's voice signature.
  • One voice sample — Settings → Advanced, in the samples list. Deleting the last sample deletes the whole voiceprint.
  • Access you granted someone — open the session, open its share list, remove the person. Their access ends immediately.
  • A therapist link — on the therapist card, tap Unlink. Note that unlinking stops future sharing; it does not retroactively un-share sessions already granted. Remove those from each session's share list.

Email a deletion request

Or write to sagearbor@gmail.com with the subject "MindShift account deletion" and the email address on the account.

There is no self-serve "delete my account" button in the app yet. This is a manual request handled by a person, and we would rather say so than imply a button that does not exist.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account itself.
  • Every stored recording and session: audio, video, transcripts, analysis, titles and speaker labels.
  • Your voiceprint and every enrolled person's voice signature.
  • Your therapist link and any share grants you made or received.
  • Your saved settings, relationships and voice profile.
  • Diagnostic reports tied to your account.

What does not get deleted, and why

  • Notes a therapist wrote about a session are that therapist's own record, held under their account. Deleting your account removes their access to your sessions; their notes stay with them.
  • A copy on someone else's phone. If you exported a session or someone screenshotted it, we cannot reach that.
  • Videos in your own camera roll. Videos recorded in the app are also saved to your phone's photo library. That copy is yours — delete it from your phone's gallery.
  • Operational logs (timestamps, error codes, account identifiers — never transcripts or audio) may persist in server logs for a short period before rotating out.

How long it takes

Within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner. We reply to confirm when it is done. If you do not hear back within 30 days, email again — it means the first one did not arrive.
