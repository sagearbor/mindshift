Notes

  • Watch modes: Standard and Battery Saver listen locally and stream only on loud moments. Session streams everything. Companion has no mic and buzzes on the phone's cues. If offline, the watch falls back to its own loudness buzzes.
  • Apple Watch: Companion mode buzzes only. Its own mic sessions are not built.
  • Cost drivers: cloud speech-to-text (when the phone's fails), Claude calls (only when triggered), and the always-on server holding the 1.3 GB tone model.
  • Open questions: the Gemini Nano step versus the no-Gemini rule; whether a grey licence is OK for odyssey; files on the phone instead of the cloud.
