# Audio tone, round 2 — per-speaker normalization, dimensional models, fusion (2026-08-24)

Follow-up to [round 1](2026-08-24-wav2vec2-iemocap-eval.md), which measured
SpeechBrain's wav2vec2-IEMOCAP classifier at 40% 4-class / 50% arousal on our
acted fixtures and shipped it dark: it read voice identity, not emotion.
Round 1 named three next steps — per-speaker logit normalization, a
naturalistic dimensional (arousal/valence) model, and fusion with prosody +
text-tone. This round measures all three on the full labeled pack and wires
the winner into `server/tone_id.py` behind `MINDSHIFT_TONE_BACKEND`.

All tables are generated by `scripts/tone_eval.py --backend all --fusion`
and live in [`round2/tables.md`](round2/tables.md); every number below is
in [`round2/summary.json`](round2/summary.json), and the raw per-turn model
outputs (`round2/raw_<backend>.json`) let the analysis be re-run without the
models. Machine: arm64 macOS 15.6, python 3.12, torch 2.13, 4 threads, CPU.

## The question changed

The product does not need a 4-way emotion label. It needs to know when the
**user's own voice is escalating** — the expected-nudge timeline in the scene
metas is exactly "self turns whose delivery heats up". Round 1's failure was
that every backend's raw per-turn output is dominated by *which voice* is
speaking. So round 2 scores every backend on **escalation** (truth = coarse
label `angry`, 19 of 65 turns) three ways:

- **raw** — the backend's per-turn arousal number as-is (arousal dimension,
  or for a 4-class model the angry-vs-best-other logit margin);
- **delta** — that number minus the same speaker's running **median over
  their previous turns** (`tone_id.EscalationTracker`; causal, i.e. what a
  live session can know; a speaker's first turn is unscored);
- **zspk** — z-scored per speaker over the whole fixture (the offline upper
  bound for "per-speaker normalization").

AUC is threshold-free. Accuracy is **leave-one-fixture-out**: the threshold
is chosen on four fixtures and applied to the fifth, then pooled — never on
the turn it scores. "Pinned" is the constant the server actually runs at
(`tone_id.ESCALATION_DELTA_THRESHOLD`), reported in-sample and labelled so.

## What the numbers say

1. **Raw per-turn tone is near chance for every model, big or small, acted
   or naturalistic** (AUC 0.53–0.61). Round 1's "reads the voice, not the
   emotion" holds for all four checkpoints. The per-fixture-speaker table in
   `round2/tables.md` shows the raw signal flags 2/2 angry turns of the
   `onyx`/gptaudio-A voice and 0/2 of the other voice in both round-1
   fixtures — the same bias, four models.

2. **The per-speaker DELTA is the signal.** Subtracting each speaker's own
   running median turns odyssey_dim's 0.61 into **0.81 AUC / 83% LOSO**, and
   lifts every other backend by +0.1–0.2 AUC. The causal version (previous
   turns only) is as good as the offline z-score or better, is usable from a
   speaker's **second** turn (k=1: 0.76 / 82%) and saturates by five. This
   is the cheapest possible fix and it is what shipped: `EscalationTracker`
   is pure numpy, per session, per speaker.

3. **Only odyssey_dim clears the bar with a margin over volume.** The
   owner's rule is "≥ ~60%, and volume-only is not acceptable". RMS-dB
   delta alone is 0.68 AUC / 74%; superb_er's delta (0.75 / 74%) and
   msp_dim's (0.72 / 71%) are not distinguishable from it on 65 turns.
   odyssey_dim's 0.81 / 83% is, and at -20 dB its delta AUC holds at 0.72
   (raw drops to 0.57) — the model is reading more than level, though not
   perfectly level-invariant. The pinned threshold 0.03 (the LOSO folds
   chose 0.015–0.03; 0.03 taken for precision) gives 85% / 74% precision /
   74% recall, self nudges 5 hit / 1 miss / 1 false.

4. **It does NOT beat text-tone, and fusion adds noise-level lift.** The
   product's own prompt through `claude-haiku-4-5` scores **86% / 0.90 AUC**
   on the same turns with the same self-nudge timeline (5/1/1). Text +
   odyssey delta is 85% CV (0.83) vs text alone 83% (0.82): +2–3 points, and
   a 5000-sample paired bootstrap over turns puts the difference at +3.1
   points with a **95% CI of [−3.1, +9.2]** (P(lift ≤ 0) = 0.22). A simple
   OR-rule (text escalated OR audio delta ≥ 0.08) gives 86% and the best
   self-nudge line of anything measured (6 / 0 / 1), but that is one turn's
   difference on this pack.

5. **Where audio helps and where it can't** (per-turn comparison, text rule
   vs odyssey delta at 0.08): both right 47, text-only right 9, audio-only
   right 4, both wrong 5. Audio's four unique wins are exactly the cases the
   feature exists for — two *rising* turns whose words are mild (gptaudio
   `defensive_rising`: text frustration 38; couple `tense_rising`: text 52/58,
   just under the 60 rule) and two text false alarms where defensive words
   were delivered calmly (`calm_neutral` "the car was six hundred", and a
   `hurt_sad` plea). Audio's unique misses: **all three `cold_contempt`
   turns** (quiet delivery — a delta on arousal cannot catch contempt; text
   caught every one because the scripts make contempt lexical) and two
   first-turn `calm_guarded` false alarms.

6. **The fixtures favour text.** These are TTS-acted scripts in which the
   emotion is written into the words (the shout is in caps, contempt says
   "beneath you", repair says "I'm sorry"). Real fights are full of "fine."
   said five ways. A pack where words and delivery disagree is the missing
   measurement — see "what would flip it on".

7. **First real-audio check is encouraging but unlabeled.** On
   `family_real` (owner + son, ~30 s, real phone audio) odyssey_dim flags
   turns 5–7 and not 3. The transcript: turn 5 *"Wait. What? I don't wanna
   go to bike camp. What?"*, turn 6 (owner) *"…and I'm arguing now"*, turn 7
   *"…dad. Stop. Stop."* — the mock argument the owner staged, with valence
   dropping 0.47 → 0.31 on those turns. Round 1's report called this clip
   "calm, ordinary speech"; it isn't after 16 s. superb_er additionally
   flags the calm turn 3 (a false alarm); iemocap flags 3, 5, 6. This is
   one recording and the owner's word, not ground truth — but it is the
   behaviour we want and it came from real audio, not TTS. `poker6_real`
   has one turn per speaker so nothing is scored (no baselines).

8. **Per-speaker z-scoring the round-1 model's logits** (the very cheapest
   idea) lifts its 4-class from 40% to 45% and its arousal AUC from 0.53 to
   0.59 — real but not enough. The IEMOCAP checkpoint's problem is not
   calibration; its logits carry little escalation information (delta 0.66).

9. **License and size.** The audeering MSP model that round 1 suggested is
   CC-BY-NC-SA and its card says "research purpose only" — it was measured
   (msp_dim row) and is deliberately **not** a `tone_id` backend. The MIT
   Odyssey-2024 baseline (`3loi/SER-Odyssey-Baseline-WavLM-Multi-Attributes`,
   MSP-Podcast, WavLM-large) is the shippable dimensional model — and it
   measured better. Its cost is real: 1.27 GB on disk, **~3 GB RSS**, ~280 ms
   per turn here (RTF 0.06). The Cloud Run service is deployed at
   `--memory 2Gi`, so this backend needs a 4Gi instance if it is ever turned
   on. Dynamic int8 quantization of the FFN + head Linears (WavLM's attention
   reads `q_proj.bias` directly and can't be quantized that way) kept the
   delta AUC (0.815) but ran **3× slower** on this CPU (qnnpack), so it was
   dropped. superb_er (0.45 GB RSS) fits 2Gi but, per (3), is a volume
   detector in disguise.

## Decision

- `MINDSHIFT_TONE_BACKEND` **default = `odyssey_dim`**: the only backend
  that clears ~60% on escalation with a clear margin over volume-only, MIT
  licensed, revisions pinned. `superb_er` and `iemocap` stay selectable
  (round-1 numbers reproducible; the 2 GiB option on record).
- `MINDSHIFT_TONE_AUDIO` **default stays `dark`** in code and **`off` in
  `deploy_cloudrun.sh`**. The owner's rule is explicit: no lift over
  text-tone → dark. Audio-delta escalation is genuinely good (83%) but it
  does not beat text (86%) and the fusion lift (+2–3 points) is inside the
  noise of 65 turns; paying 3 GB RSS per instance for that is not
  justified today. In `dark` the server now logs the number that matters
  (`arousal=… delta=… history=…` per turn), so a future real-audio
  calibration has data.
- The label vocabulary the backend emits is honest by construction: a
  dimensional turn is `unscored` (no baseline yet) / `steady` / `escalating`
  — never a fabricated `angry`; `live_sessions.ESCALATION_LABELS` treats
  `escalating` as an escalation so nothing downstream needs to change when
  the flag flips.

## What would flip it on (in order)

1. **A "words ≠ delivery" pack**: 20–30 turns where the transcript is
   neutral and the delivery is not (and vice versa), acted or real. That is
   the only place audio can show lift over text, and this pack has four
   such turns — all of which audio got and text missed.
2. **Real recordings labelled by the owner** (family_real is the proof the
   plumbing works on phone audio): a few real disagreements with a per-turn
   "was I heating up?" label would set the threshold on a real noise floor
   instead of TTS flatness (the LOSO folds picked 0.015–0.03; real speech
   moves more between turns — family_real's calm turn 3 sits at −0.05, its
   argument turns at +0.10 to +0.32).
3. **The phone's actual text-tone**, not cloud Haiku: the baseline here is
   the strongest text model the product can call. If the on-device local
   LLM measures materially worse, audio-delta's relative value goes up.
4. Cheaper inference if it goes on: ONNX export of the WavLM backbone, or a
   smaller SSL backbone fine-tuned on MSP-Podcast dims (none published under
   a usable license as of this eval).

## Reproduce

```
export MINDSHIFT_TONE_CACHE=server/.tone_cache   # gitignored; ~2.3 GB for all backends
tmp/venv-voice/bin/python scripts/tone_eval.py --backend all --fusion
tmp/venv-voice/bin/python scripts/tone_eval.py --report-only --fusion   # from cached raw JSON
tmp/venv-voice/bin/python scripts/tone_eval.py --refresh-text            # re-query the LLM baseline
```

Live-gated tests pin the measured behaviour on the canonical scene
(`server/tests/test_tone_id.py::test_live_odyssey_couple_escalation_timeline`:
self flagged on exactly turns 4/6/8, partner's plea at 7 the one false
alarm; `test_live_superb_er_schema_and_measured_flags`) and run only when the
pinned snapshots are in the cache.
