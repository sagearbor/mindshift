Bottom line.
  • Nudge judges: the disagreement was mostly one judge. gpt-5-mini (the old judge B) is a lenient outlier and is not even consistent with itself (kappa 0.39 against its own earlier verdicts on the same 17 lines; Sonnet 5.5 reproduced its own exactly). Three judges from three vendors, Claude Haiku 5.5 + GPT-6.1 Terra + Gemini 3.8 Flash, agree on 92-97% of lines (Fleiss 0.60 on 110 lines, 0.67 on 70 fresh lines). All six judges graded all 28 rule-violation cases correctly.
  • But the pre-registered bars failed, and the core panel is not confirmed. All six judges together: Fleiss 0.13. The fresh-set check missed its CI bar (lower bound -0.02) because only 3-5 of 70 held-out lines were called helpful. Too few good lines exist to prove agreement on what is good. Next step: a case set with more good lines.
  • Emotion: no audio LLM we can reach is a trustworthy emotion labeller. On CREMA-D, where one human rater scores kappa 0.48, Gemini 3.8 Flash scores 0.16 (it calls 66% of clips "neutral"), Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 0.24 and GPT-audio-1.5 0.07. GPT-audio and Flash-Lite label the words, not the voice (99% and 72% follow a contradicting transcript). Gemini 3.8 Flash follows the words 13% of the time (WARN).
  • emotion2vec+ looks human-level (0.56), but it matches the actors' intended labels at kappa 0.92, well above the crowd's own agreement, and gets 94% on RAVDESS. That points to both corpora being in its training data, and its licence is ambiguous, so use it for evaluation only.
  • Soft labels: every labeller's probabilities are further from the crowd's vote shares than two halves of the crowd are from each other. All fail the soft-label bar.
